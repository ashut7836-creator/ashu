Mercury

A guide to Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest to the Sun. Its heavily cratered surface resembles Earth's Moon, but its large iron-rich core makes it unusually dense.

Average distance from Sun58 million km
Rotation period58.6 Earth days

Temperature−180 to 430 °C
Planet typeTerrestrial planet

Atmosphere

Mercury has an extremely thin exosphere rather than a substantial atmosphere. Atoms are released from its surface by sunlight and the solar wind, so there is little air to retain heat.

Surface and composition

Its rocky crust is marked by impact craters, smooth volcanic plains and long cliffs formed as the planet cooled and contracted. Some permanently shadowed polar craters may contain water ice.

Rotation and orbit

Mercury completes an orbit in about 88 Earth days, but rotates slowly. One sunrise-to-sunrise solar day lasts about 176 Earth days.

Moons and rings

Mercury has no known natural moons and no rings.

Space exploration

Mariner 10 flew past Mercury in the 1970s. NASA's MESSENGER orbited the planet from 2011 to 2015 and mapped its surface. BepiColombo is travelling to study Mercury in greater detail.

Quick facts about Mercury

  • Smallest planet in the solar system
  • A solar day lasts longer than its year
  • Water ice may survive in permanently shadowed polar craters
