Venus

A guide to Venus

Venus is nearly as large as Earth, but its environment is extreme. Thick clouds hide the surface, while a powerful greenhouse effect makes it the hottest planet.

Atmosphere

A dense atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide, with clouds containing sulfuric acid. Surface pressure is about 90 times Earth's sea-level pressure.

Surface and composition

Radar mapping reveals volcanic plains, highlands and many volcanic features. Its surface is rocky, hot and dry; thick clouds prevent ordinary visible-light views from space.

Rotation and orbit

Venus rotates very slowly and in the opposite direction to most planets. Its rotation period is longer than its 225-day trip around the Sun.

Moons and rings

Venus has no known natural moons and no rings.

Space exploration

The Soviet Venera landers returned the first photographs from the surface. NASA's Magellan spacecraft mapped much of Venus with radar; new missions are planned.

Quick facts about Venus

  • Hottest planetary surface in the solar system
  • Rotates backwards compared with most planets
  • Brightest planet in Earth's night sky
