Jupiter

A guide to Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet. It is so massive that all the other planets together still contain less mass. Jupiter is a gas giant with no solid surface to stand on.

Atmosphere

Its atmosphere is mostly hydrogen and helium. Powerful winds create alternating light and dark bands, while the Great Red Spot is a long-lived storm larger than Earth.

Surface and composition

There is no solid ground. Pressure and temperature rise deeper inside, where hydrogen becomes a dense fluid. Its deep interior helps generate a powerful magnetic field.

Rotation and orbit

Jupiter rotates very quickly, completing a day in about ten hours. It takes nearly 12 Earth years to complete one orbit.

Moons and rings

Jupiter has many known moons. The four largest are Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto. It also has faint, thin rings.

Space exploration

Pioneer and Voyager flew past Jupiter, Galileo orbited it, and NASA's Juno spacecraft studies the planet from a polar orbit.

Quick facts about Jupiter

  • Largest planet in the solar system
  • The Great Red Spot is a giant storm
  • Europa may hide a global ocean beneath ice
