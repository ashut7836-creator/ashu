Earth

A guide to Earth

Earth is our home and the only world currently known to support life. Liquid-water oceans, a protective atmosphere and a magnetic field help make its surface habitable.

Atmosphere

The atmosphere is about 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen, with small amounts of other gases. It moderates temperature, supports weather and helps shield the surface from harmful radiation.

Surface and composition

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. Continents and ocean floors sit on moving tectonic plates that build mountains and open ocean basins over time.

Rotation and orbit

Earth's axis is tilted about 23.4 degrees, producing the seasons. One rotation makes a day, and one orbit around the Sun makes a year.

Moons and rings

Earth has one natural moon. Its gravity is the main driver of ocean tides. Earth has no ring system.

Space exploration

Satellites observe weather, oceans and climate. Astronauts conduct research aboard the International Space Station, while robotic spacecraft explore the Moon and other destinations.

Quick facts about Earth

  • Only known planet with life
  • Oceans cover about 71% of the surface
  • One large natural moon
